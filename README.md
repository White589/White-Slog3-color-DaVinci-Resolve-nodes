# White-Slog3-color-DaVinci-Resolve-nodes
It is well known that Sony's S-Log3 gamma curve offers powerful color grading flexibility. If you are shooting with a Sony Alpha mirrorless camera released after 2020 that records 10-bit color — such as the a7 IV, a7S III, a7R V, a7C II, a1, a6700, ZV-E1, or ZV-E10 II — you will have plenty of room to shape the image in DaVinci Resolve. The footage holds up well to contrast, saturation, and creative stylization without breaking down as easily as 8-bit log. The S-Log3 workflow also plays an important role in modern film and hybrid shooting pipelines. That said, if you are an experienced colorist, this may be unnecessary, and you might not even use this node setup. But if you are just getting started with Sony mirrorless cameras, color grading can feel intimidating. This node structure is designed to save you time and give you a clean starting point. The Beta Version centers on a single node set: "STD", a neutral, standard reconstruction that serves as your clean grading baseline. It is available in Simplified Chinese and English. The node was tested on the Sony a6700 & a7m4, and it should also work with other Sony Alpha mirrorless cameras that support 10-bit recording, though minor adjustments may be needed. All nodes assume an S-Log3 "expose to the right" workflow. When using a gray card, reference the 18% middle-gray exposure and overexpose neutral gray by approximately +1.3 to +1.7 EV to keep the shadows clean and maintain grading headroom.
